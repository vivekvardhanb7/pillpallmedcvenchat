## 1. Principles of Substitution in PILLPAL

Autonomous medication substitution by algorithmic systems carries significant clinical and legal risk. PILLPAL enforces the following strict rules:

1. **Generic Bioequivalence (Level 1 Substitution):**
   - Permissible *only* when the alternative is registered in the official Moroccan Generic Directory (RMMG) as an approved bioequivalent generic containing the identical active molecule, identical salt/ester, identical strength, and identical release form.
   - Example: Substituting `DOLIPRANE 500 mg effervescent` with an approved Moroccan generic `PARAFEBRYL 500 mg effervescent`.
2. **Formulation / Strength Changes (Level 2 Substitution):**
   - The system must explicitly explain differences to the patient (e.g. tablet vs effervescent solution, or splitting a scored tablet).
   - Never substitute modified-release (extended-release, CR, SR, ER) formulations with immediate-release formulations without explicit pharmacist clearance.
3. **Therapeutic Class Swapping (Level 3 Substitution):**
   - Swapping drug classes (e.g., Paracetamol to Ibuprofen) is **NOT** a direct substitution. It constitutes a completely new clinical evaluation requiring full contraindication re-screening.
4. **Patient Autonomy:**
   - The patient has the absolute right to decline any suggested alternative.
   - If the patient declines, PILLPAL immediately terminates the substitution pathway, states that the requested original brand is unavailable in this kiosk, and offers directions to the nearest full-service retail pharmacy.
