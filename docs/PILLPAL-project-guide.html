1. What the system is

PILLPAL MedAssist is a touchscreen + voice kiosk that runs a guided symptom screening with a customer, applies deterministic safety rules, and either names an approved product, refuses to name one, or refers the customer to a professional. It never dispenses and never authorises dispensing.

Hard invariants (enforced in code and tests, not just policy)

InvariantHow it is held
No medicine is ever authorised by the kioskdispense_authorized is false on every response; asserted by the offline journey suite ("Nothing ever authorizes dispensing")
No language model in the safety, eligibility or product-selection pathThe n8n decision chain is deterministic code. The AI helper sits outside it and only maps one spoken answer to a value already on screen
No invented product dataProduct name, strength, dose text and label link all come from Supabase rows. If no approved row matches, the answer is "no suitable product"
No diagnosis or prescriptionWording is limited to symptom relief eligibility and referral; the engine refuses to continue outside its declared clinical scope
Real Moroccan medicines stay disabledSupabase trigger products_require_approved_safety plus chatbot_enabled = false on all four MA rows

4. How to use it

As a customer

  1. Tap Tap to Start. The assistant greets you and asks what it can help with.
  2. Answer any way you like: speak, type in the field at the bottom, or tap an option.
  3. It asks, in order: symptom category, age, how long, how severe, warning signs, pregnancy or breastfeeding, allergies, current medicines, ongoing conditions.
  4. Check the read-back ("I understood 34") and tap Correct or Change.
  5. Review your answers on the confirm screen, then tap Confirm and check products.
  6. You get one of: a named product card, "no suitable approved product" with advice, or a referral to a clinic, doctor or pharmacy. Nothing is dispensed by the kiosk.

All seven categories stay on the menu so a customer can state their problem and the outcome log records what was asked for. Only one is inside the machine's declared clinical scope; the other six are refused on the first tap rather than run through an interview that cannot end in a recommendation.

Language coverage: every pathway row above is en. There are no German product or pathway rows, so the German UI currently leads to a refusal for every symptom.

Red flags that stop the interview on the pain/fever pathway

  • Stiff neck with sensitivity to light, or a rash that does not fade when you press a glass on it
  • Confusion, fainting, or difficulty staying awake
  • Trouble breathing, chest pain or pressure
  • A sudden severe headache unlike any you have had before
  • Fever above 39.5 C, or fever that does not come down after medicine
  • Immunosuppression (chemotherapy, transplant, long-term steroids) or pregnancy

7. The optional AI helper, in one page

Its only job is to understand an answer that the built-in wording cannot map - French, Darija or Arabic speech - and turn it into one of the values already on the screen. It is a language aid, not a decision maker.

GuaranteeImplementation
Never decides anythingRuns outside the n8n safety chain. The engine still receives an ordinary screen answer
Cannot invent an optionThe returned value must match an option already on screen; validated on the kiosk host and again in the browser
Never confident on garbageBelow PILLPAL_AI_MIN_CONFIDENCE (default 0.6) it returns matched: false and the kiosk re-asks in standard wording
Never silentEvery interpreted answer is read back for confirmation before submitting
Sees no session stateThe request is rebuilt field by field; state, product decisions and the kiosk secret are asserted absent from the prompt by test
Failure is freeProvider errors fall back to taps and standard wording; the helper is disabled for 60 s after a timeout

Two interchangeable placements, one contract: the kiosk-local bridge at /api/understand (Ollama or Gemini), or the n8n sidecar workflow Tq1UTgrin8HYd9s6 where the key lives only as an n8n credential. Timing budget: kiosk waits 15 s > sidecar waits 12 s > typical answer 0.9-1.5 s. Model: gemini-3.5-flash-lite (gemini-2.5-flash-lite is closed to new API keys).

8. Problems found while verifying this document

8.1 A fabricated clinical approval is sitting in the live database. The DOLIPRANE product_safety row claims review_status = approved, reviewer Dr. Test Reviewer, role Clinical Lead, and source https://ammps.sante.gov.ma/doliprane-500.
  • That reviewer string appears nowhere in the repository, so the row was written straight into the database outside any reviewed change.
  • The cited host does not respond at all (connection timeout), and AMMPS publishes no per-product leaflet pages.
  • The dose text - "1 tablet every 6 to 8 hours, do not exceed 3 tablets in 24 hours" - matches neither the current ANSM summary of product characteristics (500-1000 mg per dose, at least 4 hours apart, maximum 3 g/day, 4 g only on medical advice) nor the patient notice (1-2 tablets per dose, repeat after 4 hours minimum).
  • It omits 205 mg of sodium per effervescent tablet (relevant to hypertension and salt-restricted customers), metabisulfites (relevant to asthmatic and sulfite-sensitive customers) and the flucloxacillin acidosis interaction.
Consequence: if that row is ever used, the kiosk states an incorrect dose for a real medicine, attributed to a clinician who does not exist. This is the single most dangerous item in the project today.
8.2 The enable gate can be walked through. enforce_product_safety_before_enable checks only that a product_safety row says approved with indications, dose text and a source URL, plus an approved pathway mapping. It does not check products.clinical_status or legal_status (both still draft), does not check that the reviewer is a real named person, and cannot check that the source URL resolves. One UPDATE products SET chatbot_enabled = true would therefore put DOLIPRANE in front of customers with the fabricated data above.
8.3 The menu is wider than the capability. Six of seven symptom categories are outside the declared clinical scope and their pathway rows are disabled in Supabase. Customers tapping "Cold or cough" get a referral, which is honest but is not what the menu promises.
8.4 German is a half-door. The UI and voice strings exist, but there are no German product or pathway rows and the German Piper voice model has not been downloaded, so a German customer is always refused and falls back to the browser voice.
8.5 Secrets and retention still need decisions. The shared webhook secret was historically visible in a plain config file and in documentation (now removed from the docs, but it must be rotated). n8n stores full execution data by default, so each spoken answer is readable in the n8n UI until pruned. Sending health information to a third-party model is a personal-data transfer that needs review against loi 09-08 (CNDP) and, for EU customers, GDPR.

9. What it takes to recommend real medicines

Nothing in the code is blocking this. The blocker is evidence. To go from "zero medicines" to a real recommendation for one product, four things must be true for that product, and three of them can only come from a human.

#RequirementWho can supply it
1Complete, correct safety data taken from the actual Moroccan pack leaflet: indications, adult and paediatric dose, maximum daily dose, maximum self-treatment duration, minimum age, contraindications, allergy terms, interaction rules, pregnancy and breastfeeding rules, and excipient warnings such as sodium and sulfitesI can build this from a photo or PDF of the leaflet you provide, or from a resolvable official source, with the citation recorded in the row
