How to take & precautions
Adult dose shown: ages 18+ and weight 50 kg or more. Under 18 or under 50 kg: ask a pharmacist for the appropriate product and dose.
Start with 1 tablet (500 mg); use 2 only if 1 does not relieve symptoms. Wait at least 4 hours between doses. Swallow with water. Maximum 6 tablets (3,000 mg) in 24 hours without medical advice. Seek advice if fever lasts over 3 days, pain over 5 days, or symptoms worsen.
medication
Do not take if you are allergic to paracetamol or any ingredient in the medicine.
warning
Do not take with other medicines containing paracetamol, including some cold and flu remedies.
local_pharmacy
Ask a pharmacist first for liver/kidney problems, weight under 50 kg, or other medicines.
